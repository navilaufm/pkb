Create Reusable GIS Workflows with GeoLibre Model Builder and AI Assistant

In this tutorial, I demonstrate how to create, run, save, and reuse graphical GIS workflows directly in your browser. We start with a simple vector workflow, then build a complete hydrological workflow that extracts stream networks from a DEM.

You will also see how GeoLibre's AI Assistant can automatically build the entire workflow from a natural-language prompt, saving you from manually finding and connecting individual geoprocessing tools.

GeoLibre includes more than 1,000 geoprocessing tools and runs entirely in your browser using WebAssembly. It is free and open source, with no installation required.

Video tutorial: [youtu.be/dzjNKM6slgs](http://youtu.be/dzjNKM6slgs)

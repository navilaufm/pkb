## Frequently asked questions

### FAQ

Do I need a Mac?

No. Everything runs from a web browser on any OS — including building, previewing, and publishing.

Is the output real native code?

Yes. The agent writes real Swift code you can read, edit, and ship. What goes to the App Store is a genuine native binary — and the Android build is a native APK from the same code.

Can I use my own ChatGPT subscription?

Yes! It's the recommended way to run Dactyl. Connect the plan you already pay for, and the AI runs on it; Dactyl never resells you tokens, and failed builds cost nothing. No subscription? The free tier runs on included credits instead.

How is Dactyl different from other AI app builders?

Most generate React Native inside a wrapper, preview through an app you install (or a paid cloud simulator), and resell AI tokens at a markup. Dactyl writes real SwiftUI, previews instantly in the browser (or on your device), and can run on your own ChatGPT plan.

What can the agent build?

Anything you can describe. Chat apps, social feeds, games, trackers, productivity tools—all with real device capabilities when the idea needs them: camera, maps, GPS, and AI.

Do my apps get a backend?

Yes, built-in and zero-setup: user accounts (including real Sign in with Apple and Google—no keys to configure), saved data, realtime sync, and AI features. It works identically in the browser preview and the shipped app.

Can I charge money in my app?

Yes! Real StoreKit in-app purchases are built in. Declare your products once and they work in the preview, then carry through to App Store Connect when you publish.

Does Dactyl build Android apps?

Yes; the same Swift compiles into a native Android app (no rewrite, no webview) you install straight from a QR code. Play Store publishing is coming later.

Can I publish to the App Store?

Yes! Submit straight from the editor, with provisioning, listings, and review handled with you. Updates ship over the air.

Do I need an Apple Developer account?

For QR installs—iPhone, iPad, or Android—no. For TestFlight and the App Store, Apple requires you to have an Apple Developer account ($99/yr from Apple).

Do I need to know Swift, or know how to code?

No. You describe, the agent writes. But the Swift code output is yours: readable, editable, and exportable as an Xcode project anytime, so there is no lock-in.

What does it cost?

Dactyl is free to try. The Builder plan is $20/mo, with your own ChatGPT subscription powering the AI. See Pricing for the napkin math.

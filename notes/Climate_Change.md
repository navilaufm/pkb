# Climate Change

**Summary**: Notes on climate risk, extreme weather events, and related datasets and research.
**Last updated**: 2026-08-27

---

- [Central America CMORPH SPI 3-Month Average](https://ftp.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/International/dm/Central_America_CMORPH_SPI_3_mo_avg.png): NOAA Climate Prediction Center map of the 3-month Standardized Precipitation Index (SPI) for Central America, derived from CMORPH satellite-gauge precipitation estimates, used to visualize drought/wet-spell severity across the region. keywords: SPI, drought monitoring, CMORPH, NOAA CPC, Central America, precipitation anomaly
- [GPM Daily](https://climateengine.org/datasets/climatehydrology/gpm_daily_11000/#terms-of-use): Climate Engine documentation for the Global Precipitation Measurement (GPM) IMERG dataset — 11km, daily, global precipitation and SPI coverage from 2000–present, sourced from the NASA/JAXA GPM [[Remote_Sensing|satellite mission]] and accessible via Earth Engine assets. keywords: GPM, IMERG, precipitation, Climate Engine, satellite data, SPI, NASA
- [CHC Early Estimates](https://chc.ucsb.edu/monitoring/early-estimates): UC Santa Barbara Climate Hazards Center tool for monitoring recent rainfall conditions (1–18 pentad accumulations) across Africa using CHIRPS3, preliminary CHIRPS, and CHIRPS-GEFS 15-day forecasts, with total, anomaly, percent-of-average, SPI, and rank map products by region. keywords: CHIRPS, rainfall monitoring, drought early warning, SPI, Africa, Climate Hazards Center
- [CHIRPS-GEFS](https://www.chc.ucsb.edu/data/chirps-gefs): Bias-corrected, downscaled 5–16 day precipitation forecasts from NCEP's GEFS, quantile-matched to the CHIRPS historical distribution; used by CHC Early Estimates, the GEOGLAM Crop Monitor, and national met agencies for flood/drought early warning and [[Agriculture|agro-climatological]] outlooks. keywords: CHIRPS-GEFS, precipitation forecast, drought early warning, GEFS, bias correction, flood forecasting
- [Drought.gov Data Download](https://www.drought.gov/data-download): Download portal for standardized drought indices and climate datasets (precipitation, temperature, soil moisture, SPI, SPEI, EDDI) from NOAA, NASA, and partners, in GeoTIFF, XYZ tile, and [[Data|GeoJSON/cloud-optimized]] formats for GIS analysis and decision-making across agriculture, water management, and wildfire. keywords: drought indices, SPI, SPEI, EDDI, GeoTIFF, GIS data, NOAA, NASA
- [Meteo.Tech: Agentic Weather Intelligence](https://meteo.tech/): Next-gen agentic intelligence for risk management, combining proprietary 5km WRF weather models with proactive [[Agentic_Coding|AI agents]] that turn forecasts into real-time operational alerts (e.g. "suspend high-altitude work by 14:00") for [[Agriculture]], energy, logistics, construction, mining, and media across Latin America, plus pro bono pilots for smallholder farmers and disaster-response agencies. keywords: weather intelligence, risk alerts, Latin America, AI forecasting, agentic AI, WRF models, operational safety
- [Groundsource: A Dataset of Flood Events from News](https://zenodo.org/records/18647054): Open-access [[Data]] dataset containing 2.6 million historical flood events derived from news articles spanning more than 150 countries, distributed as a single Parquet file to support global flood-event research. keywords: flood events, disaster dataset, news-derived data, Parquet, global coverage, climate risk
